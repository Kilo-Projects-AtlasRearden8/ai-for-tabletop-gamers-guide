# Privacy and Safety Rules

Using AI at your table is fun and useful, but it pays to be careful. These are
the rules this guide follows, and they are good rules for anyone.

## 1. Never share personal information

Do not paste into an AI chat:

- Your real name, address, phone number, or email
- Passwords or account numbers
- Anyone else's private information (friends, family, players at your table)
- Health information about real people

Use character names and made-up details instead.

## 2. Assume chats are not private

Free AI tools may save your conversations, use them to improve their models, or
have human reviewers read them. Do not write anything in a chat that you would
not want posted on a public bulletin board.

If you want true privacy, run local AI on your own computer (for example, Ollama
with a small model). It works offline after you download it, and nothing leaves
your machine.

## 3. AI is not an expert

AI can be confident and wrong. It can invent rules, people, and events that
never existed. Always check:

- Game rules against your official rulebook
- Rulings against your gamemaster
- Any important fact before you rely on it

## 4. This is education only

This guide and its worksheets are for learning and entertainment. They are not
legal, medical, financial, or professional advice. If you need help with real
paperwork, health, or money, talk to a qualified person.

## 5. Respect copyright

Do not paste full rulebooks, adventures, or copyrighted stories into an AI
chatbot. Do not ask AI to reproduce copyrighted characters, monsters, or text.
Use AI with your own ideas and your own games.

## 6. People come first

AI is a helper for the humans at your table. If the group prefers no AI, that
is fine — play the way that is fun for everyone.

## Quick privacy checklist

- [ ] No real names, addresses, or phone numbers in chats
- [ ] No passwords or account details
- [ ] No copyrighted rulebook text
- [ ] Important rules double-checked in the rulebook
- [ ] Group is comfortable using AI

Questions or concerns about privacy? Read the FAQ (`docs/10-faq.md`) or start
with local AI so nothing leaves your computer.
