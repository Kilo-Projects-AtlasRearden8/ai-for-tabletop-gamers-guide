# Getting Started with Free AI

You do not need to pay for AI to use it at your table. This section walks you
through picking a free tool, making an account (or not), and running your first
prompt.

## Step 1: Pick a free AI tool

Good free options for beginners:

- **Web chatbots with free tiers** — Claude (claude.ai), Google Gemini
  (gemini.google.com), Microsoft Copilot (copilot.microsoft.com). These work in
  your web browser and have free usage limits.
- **Local AI you run on your own computer** — Ollama (ollama.com) with a small
  model such as Llama 3.2. This runs entirely offline after you download it.
  Best for privacy and no account at all.

Start with one web chatbot. You can add local AI later if you want.

## Step 2: Make an account (web tools only)

1. Go to the site for the tool you chose.
2. Click "Sign up" or "Try for free."
3. Use an email address. You do not need to give your real name, phone number,
   or address unless the site requires it.
4. Check your email and confirm the account if asked.

No credit card is needed for the free tiers described here. If a site asks for
one to start a "free trial," close it and pick a different tool.

## Step 3: Start a new chat

1. Look for "New chat," "New conversation," or a pencil icon.
2. Type a greeting like: "Hi, I play tabletop roleplaying games. Can you help
   me make a character sheet?"
3. Press Enter and read the reply.

That is it. You are using AI.

## Step 4: Your first tabletop prompt

Copy and paste this:

> I am new to tabletop roleplaying games. I want to make a character for a
> fantasy game. Please ask me five short questions, one at a time, to help me
> build a character I will enjoy playing. Keep the language simple and
> encouraging.

Send it. Answer each question as it comes. Treat the AI like a helpful friend
who is asking so they can give better advice later.

## Step 5: Keep a "favorites" list

When the AI gives you something useful (a good NPC idea, a clear rule summary),
copy it into a notes app or notebook. Free chat histories can disappear or get
hard to find, so save anything you want to reuse.

## What to avoid as a beginner

- Do not paste your real name, home address, phone number, email, passwords, or
  anyone else's personal information into a chatbot.
- Do not paste copyrighted rulebooks or adventures into a chatbot.
- Do not treat AI output as official rules. Always check against your game's
  rulebook and your gamemaster.
