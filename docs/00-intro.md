# Welcome to AI for Tabletop RPGs and Board Games

You play games to spend time with friends, tell stories, and roll dice — not to
wrestle with spreadsheets or spend hours prepping. This guide shows you how to
use free AI tools as a friendly helper for your tabletop games, step by step,
in plain English.

It is written by Dylan Asdale, a registered nurse turned AI author and founder
of **Asdale Dynamics, LLC**. The style is the same as his book *The Massive AI
Manual* and hub site dylanasdale.github.io: AI explained "like a friend would,"
never condescending, no jargon unless it is explained first.

## What this guide covers

- Picking and setting up free AI tools (local-first options included)
- Making character sheets with AI
- Keeping campaign notes
- Brainstorming NPCs
- Looking up rule clarifications
- Preparing game sessions
- Printable worksheets you can fill out by hand
- Step-by-step prompts you can copy and paste
- Privacy rules so you stay safe while using AI

## What this guide does NOT do

This guide is education only. It does not include game rules, stat blocks,
monsters, maps, artwork, or any other game assets. Bring your own rulebooks and
your own imagination. AI is a helper, not a replacement for the gamemaster or
the group at the table.

## How to use this guide

Read it in order, or jump to the section you need today. Every section has
copy-and-paste prompts and concrete steps. Try one prompt at your next session
and see what feels useful. Ignore the rest — there is no wrong way to use a
helper.

## Where to find the HTML version

A static website version of this manual lives in `site/`. Open `site/index.html`
in any web browser. Nothing needs to be installed, and nothing phones home.
