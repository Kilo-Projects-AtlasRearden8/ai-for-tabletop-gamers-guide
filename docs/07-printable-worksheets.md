# Printable Worksheets

Two worksheets are ready to print and fill in by hand:

- `site/worksheets/character-sheet.html` — one-page character sheet
- `site/worksheets/session-worksheet.html` — session prep sheet plus a rule
  questions list

## How to print

1. Open the worksheet file in any web browser.
2. Press Ctrl+P (or Command+P on Mac) to open the print dialog.
3. Choose your printer, then print. The worksheets use a print-friendly layout
   that hides menus and colors so they print clearly on plain paper.

You can also print to PDF if you do not have a printer.

## Fill them in anytime

Use the worksheets during session prep, during the game, or afterward. They do
not need a computer, an account, or an internet connection once printed.

## Worksheet contents

**Character sheet:** name, player, game, appearance, personality, background,
strengths, flaws, goals, notes.

**Session worksheet:** date, game, players present, story so far, scenes to try,
NPCs to introduce, possible complications, questions for the gamemaster, rules
to look up after the session.
