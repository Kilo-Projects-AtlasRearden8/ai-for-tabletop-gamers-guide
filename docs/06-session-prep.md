# Session Prep with AI

Preparing for a game session means deciding what might happen, who might show
up, and what could go wrong. AI can help you brainstorm — you still decide what
actually goes at the table.

## Copy-and-paste prompt: plan a session

> I am preparing for a tabletop game session. My players are playing
> [briefly describe the characters]. The story so far: [1-2 sentences]. Please
> suggest:
> 1. One scene or encounter for the session
> 2. One NPC who could appear (name and one-line personality)
> 3. One possible complication or twist
> 4. One question to ask the players at the start
>
> Keep it short, simple, and flexible. Mark all ideas as suggestions only.

## Copy-and-paste prompt: prep a simple encounter

> Please design one simple encounter for [name of game]. Give me:
> 1. Where it happens
> 2. Who or what is involved
> 3. What could go well
> 4. What could go wrong
> 5. How to end it either way
>
> Use plain language and keep it under 200 words.

## Session prep checklist

- [ ] Know where the last session ended
- [ ] Have 1-3 possible scenes ready (not a script — players will surprise you)
- [ ] Have any NPCs named and ready
- [ ] Write down rules you want to look up
- [ ] Bring dice, character sheets, and notes
- [ ] Decide when to stop so nobody is rushed
