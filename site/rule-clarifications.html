Rule Clarifications with AI

AI can explain game rules in plain language, but it is not a rulebook and it can be wrong. Always double-check against your official rules and your gamemaster.

Copy-and-paste prompt: explain a rule

I play [name of game]. Please explain the rule for [specific rule, e.g. "how advantage works" or "how to make an attack"] in plain language, as if I am playing for the first time. Include:

  1. What the rule does
  2. When to use it
  3. A simple example with made-up numbers

Do not quote the rulebook word for word. Mark anything you are unsure about with [unsure].

Copy-and-paste prompt: compare two rules

In [name of game], what is the difference between [rule A] and [rule B]? Give a short, plain-language comparison with one example for each. Tell me if you are not certain.

Important rules about rules

  1. AI can hallucinate rules. It may state rules confidently that do not exist. Treat AI output as a suggestion, not a ruling.
  2. The gamemaster decides. At the table, the gamemaster's call is final, even if the AI said something different.
  3. Use the official rulebook first. AI is best for summarizing rules you already own, not replacing them.
  4. No copyrighted text. Do not paste full rulebook pages into a chatbot, and do not expect AI to reproduce them.

Printable help

Keep the one-page "Rule Questions" section of the session worksheet at the table to write down rules you want to look up after the session.
