Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to pay for AI?

No. All the tools and prompts in this guide work with free AI options. Web chatbots such as Claude, Gemini, and Copilot have free tiers, and local tools such as Ollama are free to download and run on your own computer.

Do I need to know how to code?

No. If you can type a message and press Enter, you can use this guide. All prompts are copy and paste.

Can AI run my game for me?

No, and it should not. AI is a helper for brainstorming, summarizing, and explaining. The gamemaster and the players run the game.

Can AI replace my rulebook?

No. AI can summarize rules in plain language, but it can be wrong. Always check the official rulebook and ask your gamemaster.

Is this safe for kids?

AI tools have their own age requirements (often 13+). An adult should set up any accounts, and kids should follow the privacy rules in Privacy & Safety — especially never sharing personal information.

Does this include game rules or monsters?

No. This guide is education only and contains no game assets, stat blocks, monsters, maps, or artwork. Bring your own games and rulebooks.
