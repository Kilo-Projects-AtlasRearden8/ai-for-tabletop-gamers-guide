Making Character Sheets with AI

AI can help you build a character from scratch, fill in details, or turn a half-formed idea into a complete sheet. It cannot replace your game's official character sheet, but it can write out everything that goes on it.

Copy-and-paste prompt: build a new character

I am making a character for a tabletop roleplaying game. I do not have the rulebook in front of me, so please do not quote exact rules or numbers.

Please give me a character concept with:

  1. A name
  2. A short personality description (2-3 sentences)
  3. A background or history (1 paragraph)
  4. Two or three things they are good at
  5. One flaw or fear
  6. One goal they want to achieve

Keep the tone friendly and the language simple. Do not include copyrighted stat blocks or game mechanics.

This gives you a story-first character. Bring it to your gamemaster to fit it into the actual rules.

Copy-and-paste prompt: describe an existing character

I have a character concept: [paste your idea here, e.g. "a clumsy wizard who loves cooking"]. Please write a one-page character summary I can hand to my gamemaster. Include appearance, personality, background, strengths, flaws, and goals. Use plain language and do not invent game mechanics or quote rules.

Tips for good character sheets

  • Start with personality, not numbers. A character you enjoy playing matters more than an optimized one.
  • Ask the AI to ask you questions first. It will learn what you like.
  • Save your favorite results in a notes app or notebook.
  • Always show AI-generated characters to your gamemaster before the session.
