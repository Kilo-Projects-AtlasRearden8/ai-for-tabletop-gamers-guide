Brainstorming NPC Ideas with AI

NPCs (non-player characters) are everyone in the game world who is not run by a player: shopkeepers, allies, rivals, quest-givers. AI is good at inventing people quickly.

Copy-and-paste prompt: create one NPC

Please create one NPC for a tabletop roleplaying game. Give me:

  1. Name
  2. Role or job
  3. One sentence describing what they look like
  4. Personality (2-3 sentences)
  5. What they want
  6. One secret they are keeping (optional, mark it clearly)
  7. One way the players could befriend or upset them

Keep the tone appropriate for a [insert your game's genre, e.g. fantasy / sci-fi / horror] game. Use plain language. Do not copy any copyrighted characters from books, movies, or games.

Copy-and-paste prompt: create several NPCs at once

Please create three different NPCs for a [village / space station / haunted house / insert your setting]. Make them different from each other in job, personality, and goals. For each, give a name, one-line appearance, personality, and what they want. Keep descriptions short (under 60 words each) and language simple.

Tips for NPCs

  • Give each NPC one memorable thing: a habit, a way of speaking, or a goal.
  • Not every NPC needs a secret. Simple people make the world feel real.
  • Let the players decide what matters. If they ignore an NPC, that is fine — reuse that NPC later or let them go.
  • AI NPC ideas are starting points. Change anything that does not fit your game.
