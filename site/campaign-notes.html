Keeping Campaign Notes with AI

Campaign notes are the memory of your game: who the players met, what happened, where the story is going. AI can help you organize them, summarize sessions, and prepare for next time.

Copy-and-paste prompt: summarize a game session

I will paste notes from a tabletop game session. Please write a short summary with:

  1. What happened (3-5 bullet points)
  2. Important NPCs the party met and what they wanted
  3. Clues, items, or promises the party now has
  4. Questions to answer next session
  5. Suggestions for what could happen next (optional, mark these clearly as AI ideas)

Keep it under 300 words and use simple language. Here are my notes:

[paste your session notes here]

Copy-and-paste prompt: organize messy notes

I have messy campaign notes below. Please organize them into:

  1. People (NPCs and players' characters)
  2. Places
  3. Events that happened
  4. Open questions and plot hooks

Use simple headings and bullet points. Do not invent new facts. If something is unclear, mark it with [unclear].

[paste your messy notes here]

Tips for campaign notes

  • Write notes during or right after the session while things are fresh.
  • Keep one document per campaign so nothing gets mixed up.
  • Label AI guesses clearly so you do not confuse them with what actually happened at the table.
  • Back up your notes (copy them to a second device or print them).

Privacy reminder: do not paste real names of people you know into campaign notes you share with an AI. Use character names only. See the Privacy & Safety page.
